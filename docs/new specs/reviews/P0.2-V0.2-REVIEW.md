## 2. Where the two amendment runs agree

- Fyers' free Standard plan is daily from 2021 and intraday from 2025; the #68 figure does not hold
  on the free plan.
- Fyers Data API limits 5, 50 and 5,000; more than 3 per-minute breaches block the day.
- A dormant-account error is permanent until a human acts, and a signals-only v1 never trades, so
  dormancy is the expected path.
- Neither broker has a licence read that permits commercial derived-signal publication. No India row
  can be SELECTED.
- Upstox is the India daily primary, with Kite out of first place.
- An Upstox rate limit is a stop-the-batch event, not a retry.
- US intraday is UNFILLED.
